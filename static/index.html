EXPERIMENTAL · OPT-IN · GET ONLY

MusicBrainz metadata pilot

Sample 20 identities per top genre and up to 50 with unknown genre. Five genres yield at most 150 entries.

Queries send selected artist/title or ISRC to MusicBrainz. No paths, audio, artwork or fingerprints are uploaded. Filename fallbacks need identity review. Audio identification would require a separate AcoustID integration.

Sequential requests use at least 1.1 seconds plus service backoff. At balanced speed, 150 uncached matches take roughly 10 minutes.

A search relevance score is never a community rating. Ratings need actual votes and may be unavailable. Review identity before staging a proposed year or genre; your personal stars are never replaced automatically. MusicBrainz supplementary data: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Cached responses stay local for up to 30 days.
