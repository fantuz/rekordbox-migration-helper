Metadata & collection coverage

Find case, spacing and Unicode variants, missing tags and values outside review thresholds. Coverage groups tracks by genre and 10-BPM bands.

Unusual tags are review candidates. Automatic cue origin is inferred from editable labels; play count zero cannot prove a track was never played.

Analysis asset audit

Inspect multiple folders or native backup ZIPs, including custom paths. Compare separate asset copies, inspect extension sizes and review STEMS evidence. Missing means absent from the chosen scopes. Unreferenced requires a complete captured reference set.

Matching analysis member names/sizes are candidates. SHA-256 verification establishes byte equality. Nothing is deleted; matching bytes alone do not establish safe deletion.

What changed between captures?

Compare exact cue positions, loops, labels, colors, personal stars and playlist membership/order. Unmatched or ambiguous tracks are reported separately; cue loss is inferred only for safe matches.

Smart-playlist criteria are compared; generated membership can change with rules and is not treated as a manual playlist edit. Snapshots preserve the source capture time.

EXPERIMENTAL · OPT-IN · GET ONLY

A small community metadata experiment.

Preview 20 distinct tag identities from each top genre, plus 50 unknown-genre tracks. Default: five genres, up to 150 tracks. Available tracks may produce a smaller sample.

Queries send selected artist/title or ISRC to MusicBrainz. No paths, audio, artwork or fingerprints are uploaded. Filename fallbacks need identity review. Audio identification would require a separate AcoustID integration.

Requests run one at a time, with a fixed 1.1-second minimum and service backoff. Two requests per uncached match are typical; 150 tracks take about 10 minutes at balanced speed, plus service delays. Other apps sharing your internet connection can also consume the service limit.

A search relevance score is never a community rating. Ratings need actual votes and may be unavailable. Review identity before staging a proposed year or genre; your personal stars are never replaced automatically. MusicBrainz supplementary data: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Cached responses stay local for up to 30 days.
